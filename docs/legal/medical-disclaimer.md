> **Read this first.** STREM is **not a medical device** and **not a substitute for
> professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.** Coaching, readiness
> scores, insights, and biomarker context are **estimates and may be wrong.**
> Always consult a qualified professional, and **in an emergency call your local
> emergency number** — do not rely on STREM.

This Medical Disclaimer is incorporated into the
[Terms of Service](terms-of-service.md) and explains the limits of the
health-related guidance STREM provides. By using the Service — and specifically
before entering biomarker or lab data — you acknowledge that you have read and
understood it.

---

## 1. STREM is not a medical device

STREM is a training, nutrition, recovery, and progression coaching app. It is **not**
a medical device, is **not** intended to diagnose, treat, cure, prevent, or monitor
any disease or medical condition, and should not be used for those purposes. It has
not been evaluated or cleared as a medical device by any regulator.

## 2. Not professional advice

The content STREM provides — including coaching from the STREM AI Race Team, training
and nutrition guidance, readiness scores, plan adjustments, insights, PR detection,
and biomarker trend context — is **general informational guidance only.** It is
**not** medical, clinical, nutritional, psychological, or other professional advice,
and it does **not** create a clinician-patient or any professional relationship.

Always seek the advice of a qualified professional (such as a physician, registered
dietitian, physiotherapist, or licensed coach) with any questions about your health,
training, nutrition, supplementation, or a medical condition. **Never disregard
professional advice, or delay seeking it, because of something STREM told you.**

## 3. Estimates may be wrong

Readiness, load, recovery, trends, and insights are **estimates** computed from the
data available — data you enter and data from sources you connect. They can be
**inaccurate, incomplete, or not applicable to you**, especially if inputs are
missing or wrong, or if you have a condition the model cannot account for. Treat
every number and suggestion as a starting point for your own judgment and a
conversation with a professional, not as a fact about your body.

## 4. Biomarkers and lab values — decision-support, never diagnosis

If you choose to enter biomarker or lab values (for example ferritin, vitamin D,
lipids, glucose/HbA1c, testosterone), STREM may surface **trends and general
context** — for example, noting that a value has moved across tests and that this is
worth discussing with a clinician.

STREM **never diagnoses, never prescribes, never interprets a result as a medical
conclusion, and always redirects you to a qualified professional.** Biomarker
analysis in STREM is **decision-support for a conversation with a professional, not
a substitute for one.** This feature is available only after you give explicit
consent and acknowledge this Disclaimer, and you can revoke it at any time.

## 5. Supplements and medications

STREM does **not** prescribe, dose, or recommend specific supplements, medications,
or products. It may provide general informational context and will flag when
something should be discussed with a professional. Decisions about supplements or
medications are between you and your clinician.

## 6. Train within your limits

Physical training carries inherent risks of injury. You are responsible for
exercising within your ability, using proper technique, warming up, and stopping if
you feel pain, dizziness, chest discomfort, shortness of breath, or other warning
signs. **Consult a qualified professional before starting or significantly changing
a training, nutrition, or supplementation program**, particularly if you are
pregnant, recovering from injury or illness, or managing a medical condition.

## 7. Emergencies

STREM is **not** an emergency service and cannot help in a crisis. If you think you
are having a medical emergency, or are in danger of harming yourself or others,
**contact your local emergency number or a crisis service immediately.** Do not wait
for, or rely on, anything in STREM.

## 8. Your acknowledgement

By accepting this Disclaimer you confirm that you understand STREM's guidance is
informational and may be wrong, that it is not a substitute for professional care,
and that you are responsible for your own health and training decisions. We surface
this Disclaimer at onboarding, again before your first biomarker entry, and in your
Profile so you can re-read it at any time.
