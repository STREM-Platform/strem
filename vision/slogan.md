# Slogan

## Primary Slogan

**STREM - Never Settle.**

## Meaning

`Never Settle` means continuous improvement without chaos. It is not a demand for perfection. It is a commitment to standards, direction, and resilience.

The slogan should imply:

- keep growing
- recover and return
- build discipline through action
- raise personal standards
- become stronger every day

It should not imply:

- burnout
- shame
- endless comparison
- ignoring recovery
- toxic productivity

## Usage

Use the slogan in high-signal brand contexts:

- app launch screen
- onboarding moments
- website hero later
- investor and partner decks
- community challenge campaigns

Do not overuse the slogan in routine UI. Daily product copy should be specific and useful.

## Supporting Lines

- `The operating system for becoming your strongest self.`
- `Structure for ambition.`
- `Train. Recover. Return stronger.`
- `Built for disciplined growth.`
