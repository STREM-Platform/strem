# User Personas

## Hybrid Athlete

**Age:** 20–40

The hybrid athlete blends strength, running, endurance, and skill work. They may train for CrossFit, obstacle races, combat sports, triathlons, mountain events, or general performance. They take training seriously and need a system that respects that.

**Goals:**
- improve across multiple domains simultaneously
- balance strength and endurance without sacrificing either
- avoid overtraining and manage recovery intelligently
- see progress clearly across all training modalities
- find and stay connected to like-minded people at a similar level

**Pain points:**
- fragmented logs across multiple apps
- unclear recovery signals (hard to know when to push vs. rest)
- difficult scheduling when balancing strength and endurance in the same week
- nutrition inconsistency across different training phases
- lack of community with genuinely similar goals (most apps skew toward purely recreational or purely elite)

**STREM value:**
- unified training and running logs across all movement modalities
- multi-pillar XP system that rewards being a complete athlete, not just a specialist
- AI coach that generates missions covering training, recovery, and nutrition together
- tribe with other serious performers
- path ranks (V2): Mountain Runner, Iron Body, Iron Mind — identity markers that reflect the actual athlete they are

**Game mechanic resonance:**
- **Rank system**: Hybrid rank is specifically named for this persona. Reaching it is identity validation.
- **Multi-pillar XP**: the XP system rewards balanced engagement across movement, nutrition, recovery, and tribe — which matches how this persona actually trains
- **Tribe challenges**: competitive tribe-vs-tribe challenges (V2) appeal strongly to this persona's competitive drive
- **Path ranks (V2)**: the Mountain Runner or Iron Body path rank gives them a specific athletic identity marker
- **Medals**: endurance milestones (First 10K, 100K Month) and strength milestones resonate deeply

## Busy Professional

**Age:** 25–50

The busy professional wants health, energy, and discipline while managing work, family, and limited time. They are not primarily athletic identity-driven, but they want to feel capable, disciplined, and healthy. Consistency is their biggest challenge.

**Goals:**
- stay consistent without requiring perfect conditions
- reduce the cognitive load of deciding what to do next
- improve body composition and energy levels
- fit training into a real schedule that doesn't always cooperate
- feel accountable to something or someone

**Pain points:**
- inconsistent routines that break under work or family pressure
- too many disconnected tools creating decision fatigue
- low time and mental bandwidth for complex planning
- nutrition friction: hard to log or plan well when busy
- difficulty restarting after missed days (shame spiral or just giving up)

**STREM value:**
- simple daily logging with minimal friction (fast log = fast XP = progress visible)
- AI next-step guidance that removes the "what do I do today?" decision
- tribe accountability from people who understand a busy schedule
- compassionate re-entry after missed days (no punishment, just return)
- weekly coaching recap that doesn't require active engagement to feel progress

**Game mechanic resonance:**
- **Missions**: the mission system removes decision fatigue — the AI tells them what matters this week across all pillars
- **Streak bonuses with no punishment**: the STREM streak model is specifically designed for this persona — streaks reward consistency but don't punish reality
- **Discipline awards**: "7-Day Commitment" and "30-Day Mark" medals resonate — they prove consistency in a life where consistency is hard
- **Tribe**: a small tribe of 4–8 people who check in briefly each day is highly compatible with limited bandwidth (no endless feeds, just accountability)
- **Re-entry missions**: when they fall off, the AI generates a small, achievable re-entry mission — this persona needs the door to be small

## Transformation Seeker

**Age:** 18–35

The transformation seeker is newer to fitness or returning after inconsistency. They want confidence, structure, and visible progress. They may feel intimidated by serious athletes but deeply want to become a different kind of person. This is the persona for whom the identity system matters most.

**Goals:**
- build a body they feel confident in (fat loss, muscle gain, or general fitness)
- build self-trust: proving to themselves they can be consistent
- understand what to actually do (they often don't know where to start)
- stay encouraged through the inevitable difficult patches
- join a group that normalizes effort and return without elite gatekeeping

**Pain points:**
- intimidation: fitness content and apps often feel designed for people who are already fit
- confusing and conflicting advice: everything online contradicts itself
- shame after missed days: the hardest part is returning after a gap
- lack of accountability: without someone watching, it's easy to stop
- unrealistic social media standards making real progress feel invisible

**STREM value:**
- onboarding that creates identity clarity, not overwhelm
- XP and rank progression that makes early progress visible (Initiate → Builder is achievable)
- AI coach that keeps next steps simple and compassionate
- tribes that normalize effort and return, not just elite achievement
- rank names that reflect real growth (Initiate, Builder, Forged) — not arbitrary numbers

**Game mechanic resonance:**
- **Early ranks**: Initiate and Builder ranks are specifically designed for this persona — they show rapid early progress and feel achievable
- **10 MVP medals**: "First Steps" and "First Mile" medals are specifically for this persona's early journey milestones
- **Compassionate streak mechanics**: no punishment for missed days means this persona can return without shame
- **Identity Forged medal**: completing full onboarding and staying active for 30 days is specifically designed as a transformation milestone for this persona
- **AI coach narrative**: the narrative coach response style ("Week 8 — you're not the same person who started in January") is most powerful for this persona, who needs to see their own change reflected back
- **Tribe**: belonging to a tribe that expects you back without judgment is the social safety net this persona needs

## Future Personas

These personas are validated directions but not MVP scope. Do not expand MVP features to serve them until retention data supports the next phase.

- **Endurance athlete**: marathon runners, triathletes, ultra-distance athletes — served well by the running system and future wearable integration
- **Martial artist**: BJJ, MMA, boxing — needs combat-sport-specific training log fields (V2)
- **Elite coach**: professional coaches managing clients — needs the coach marketplace and coach dashboard (Phase 3)
- **Youth athlete**: 14–18 age range — requires additional safety, parental consent, and age-appropriate coaching guardrails (future phase with legal review)
- **Creator or educator**: fitness content creators, coaches who also publish — needs the creator program and content tools (Phase 3+)
- **Nutrition professional**: registered dietitians who want to integrate with client tribe accountability — requires the coach ecosystem (Phase 3+)
